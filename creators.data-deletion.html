Data Deletion Instructions

Request deletion

Email hello@web-by-elie.com with the subject “Creator shop data request.” Include the purchase email and receipt reference so we can identify the relevant record. Never include card details or passwords.

What the shops hold

The shop has no customer account or password. Cloudflare stores limited delivery records with the purchased product, Stripe session reference, payment status and access expiry for 30 days. Resend processes delivery emails. Stripe holds payment and financial records under its own policies and legal obligations.

What deletion changes

Removing a delivery record may affect download recovery. Some payment or support information may need to remain for legal, accounting, fraud or dispute purposes. We will explain any applicable limits rather than promise that all third-party records are removed.

Data on your device

Offline planner notes, exported backups and downloaded files stay on your device. Clear the planner’s browser storage or delete files yourself. Website display preferences use the local storage key tilbury-display; the delivery reference uses session storage. Deleting these does not delete Stripe payment records.

Platform-controlled data

Manage Instagram, Stripe and other platform data through their own privacy tools. We can address information held by Web By Elie and coordinate relevant processor requests where applicable.
