Mail tools data deletion

Stop Google access

The account owner can remove the Desktop app connection in their Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API access. It does not delete messages from Gmail or erase previously saved working files.

Remove saved information

The integration may retain encrypted local credentials, mail extracts, drafts, attachments and business correspondence records in local or private project storage and backups. The owner must remove those copies separately and review backup retention. Messages held by Google must be managed through Gmail and Google Account controls; copies already delivered to recipients are outside this tool's control.

Ask for correction or deletion

Email hello@web-by-elie.com with the email address involved, an approximate correspondence date, and the information you want corrected or removed. Identity or ownership may need to be confirmed. Never send a password, authorization code or API token.

Requests are reviewed for the relevant records, including whether some information must remain for business or legal obligations. Where appropriate, a minimal opt-out record may be retained to prevent further contact.

Website visitors

These app-information pages have no visitor accounts, forms, payments, analytics scripts or visitor database. The privacy policy explains hosting and provider handling.
