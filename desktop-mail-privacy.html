Mail tools privacy policy

This policy covers the owner-operated Gmail integration described on the app information page, currently named Desktop app in Google OAuth. Elie Cohen operates it for his own correspondence and business activities.

Information accessed and why

After the owner authorizes Google access, the tool can access account identity, Gmail message headers, senders and recipients, message content and attachments, labels, drafts, and sent-message identifiers. It uses the information needed for the requested task: reviewing correspondence, respecting refusals and opt-outs, avoiding duplicate contact, preparing drafts, sending approved messages, and verifying results.

Storage and service providers

Mail remains in the connected Gmail account. The desktop integration stores its authorization credentials in an encrypted local provider configuration. Local working files may contain copies or extracts of correspondence, drafts, attachments, campaign records and provider receipts. Records may also be retained in private project storage and backups used by the owner.

Google processes authentication and email through its services. Where the owner asks an AI assistant to help with correspondence, relevant content may be processed by the configured assistant provider for that task. These providers also have their own terms and privacy policies. Gmail credentials, private correspondence and local campaign records are not published on this app-information website.

Limited use of Google data

The app uses Google data to provide the owner-requested mail features. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising targeting, or use it to train a general-purpose AI model. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

There is no fixed automatic deletion period for all local mail records. Records are retained while needed for correspondence, duplicate prevention and business evidence, until removed by the owner. Revoking Google access stops future authorized API access but does not itself delete existing Gmail messages, local records or backups. See the deletion instructions.

This website

These app-information pages contain no login, upload form, payment form, analytics script or advertising. The hosting service handles ordinary web requests under its own policies. Other Web By Elie pages are covered by the website privacy policy.

Contact

For a correction, deletion request or privacy question, email hello@web-by-elie.com. Do not include passwords, authorization codes or API tokens.
