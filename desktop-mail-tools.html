Desktop app — Elie's mail tools

A private desktop integration operated by Elie Cohen to manage his own email correspondence and business outreach. The Google OAuth application is currently named Desktop app.

What it does

With the account owner's Google authorization, the tool reads Gmail correspondence to check previous contact, replies and opt-outs; prepares email drafts; and sends approved messages. It retains local records of drafts and send results to avoid duplicate outreach and support follow-up.

Who can use it

This is an owner-operated tool, not a public email service. There is no visitor sign-up or Gmail connection on this website. Visiting this page does not grant access to a Google account.

Google permissions

The mail integration requests account identity, read access to Gmail, and permission to manage drafts and send email. Google presents the requested permissions during authorization. The owner can revoke access through their Google Account.

Read the privacy policy for data handling and the deletion instructions for removing access and retained records.
