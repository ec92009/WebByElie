Privacy Policy

Who operates the shops

Web By Elie operates the June Rowan, Nora Vela and Elena Brooks digital product shops. These are fictional AI presenters. Contact hello@web-by-elie.com.

Purchases and delivery

Stripe processes payments through the existing Photos By Elie merchant account used by Web By Elie. Stripe receives checkout details, including your email, payment details and information needed to process the payment. Card details are not stored by these shop pages. Stripe’s own privacy policy applies to its processing.

Cloudflare stores product files and order references used for delivery. Delivery records contain the purchased product, Stripe session reference, status and access expiry. A private link grants access for 30 days; do not share it. Resend processes your purchase email and delivery message so we can send the link. Delivery records expire after 30 days. Stripe and email providers may retain their own records under their policies; we do not promise their deletion on that date.

Your offline tools

Downloaded tools work locally. June’s planner saves notes in your browser when available; exported backups remain on your device. Elena’s edits are temporary. These tools do not send your entries to Web By Elie or external services.

Site preferences

Display preferences are stored locally. The delivery page temporarily remembers its private order reference in session storage. No advertising tracker or mailing-list signup is added by these shops. Cloudflare and Stripe may process technical information for hosting, security and payment operations.

Your choices

Contact hello@web-by-elie.com for access, correction or deletion requests. Include the purchase email and receipt reference, never card details. Required financial records may need to be retained. Manage device-local planner notes, backups and preferences on your own device.
