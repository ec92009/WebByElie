§ 05 Notes from the desk
Background & accounting
Practice note

Decades of software judgment, applied practically.

Languages spoken: English, French, and Spanish.

I have spent decades in software engineering, so I look at websites as more than a surface design problem. A good site also has structure, maintainability, search signals, tool choices, and costs underneath it.

For independent businesses, I am excited by how quickly the web is changing: AI search, better design tools, automation, and new ways to make small sites more capable without making them bloated.

Cost cleanup ledger

Where small web bills hide

  • Subscriptions

    Website apps, booking tools, email add-ons, analytics, image or AI tools nobody actively uses.

  • Overlap

    Two services doing the same job, legacy plugins kept out of habit, or a paid tier chosen years ago.

  • Renewals

    Domains, hosting, themes, plugins, and directories that renew automatically but no longer help.

  • Ownership

    Unclear logins and scattered billing that hide what is essential and what can go.

Who is Web By Elie for? +

Independent businesses that already have a website or web tools and want a practical refresh, clearer search signals, AI-readable structure, and a review of avoidable web spending.

What happens in the first pass? +

The first pass reviews the current site, preserves useful work, identifies visitor friction, compares design directions, and checks titles, descriptions, headings, service wording, links, mobile flow, AI-readable facts, and avoidable costs.

What does an AI-ready review actually test? +

Whether a person or AI assistant can identify the business, services, intended customers, locations, pricing factors, evidence, and next step without guessing. It records missing or conflicting facts and recommends corrections; it does not promise placement in an AI answer.

Can SEO or AI answer placement be guaranteed? +

No ranking, traffic, lead, or AI answer placement is guaranteed. The work improves clarity, crawlability, structure, and answer quality so the site is easier to understand.

Is the existing website replaced from scratch? +

Not by default. The process starts by preserving what works, then offers practical design and structure improvements before preparing a final direction.

How is the budget handled? +

Most projects begin with a 10-hour bundle. Work is tracked at $100 per hour with progress notes and a manual check-in when the retainer reaches 50%.
