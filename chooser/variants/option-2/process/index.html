METHOD & ACCOUNTING

How we work, track hours, and trim waste.

Standard rate: $100/hour, starting with a practical 10-hour starting bundle. Keep what already works, compare real clickable directions, and approve every step before launch.

01 // FOUR-STEP PIPELINE

From review to sign-off.

  1. 01

    Review what exists

    A short intake interview, then a review of the current site: what already works, what to preserve, and where visitors get stuck. Analytics inform the review when available.

  2. 02

    Explore possible looks

    A small palette of real design directions, so you compare actual pages instead of abstract descriptions.

  3. 03

    Choose and combine

    Pick one direction or mix pieces from several, including the existing website, until it feels right.

  4. 04

    Prepare the final site

    Tighten pages, search details, AI-ready structure, mobile flow, and practical launch items, then sign off.

02 // BUDGET STRUCTURE

Transparent hours and check-ins.

01 // Start

Most projects begin with a practical 10-hour bundle: room to review, design, and make real progress without a long proposal cycle.

02 // Accounting

Work is tracked in clear $100/hour increments, with notes on what was done and what changed.

03 // Visibility

You are kept up to date as the budget is used, with a manual check-in when the retainer reaches 50%.

03 // SAVINGS SPOTS

Where avoidable web bills usually sit.

01 // Subscriptions

Website apps, booking tools, email add-ons, analytics, image or AI tools nobody actively uses.

02 // Overlap

Two services doing the same job, legacy plugins kept out of habit, or a paid tier chosen years ago.

03 // Renewals

Domains, hosting, themes, plugins, and directories that renew automatically but no longer help.

04 // Ownership

Unclear logins and scattered billing that hide what is essential and what can go.
